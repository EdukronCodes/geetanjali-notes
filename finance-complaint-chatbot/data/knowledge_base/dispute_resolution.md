# Dispute Resolution Runbook

## Routine Query Resolution
1. Verify account and transaction in SQL Server ledger.
2. Check posting date and settlement status.
3. Respond with policy-aligned timeline and next steps.

## Complex Case Escalation
Pre-populate case summary with: intent, extracted entities, RAG context snippets, account snapshot.

## Compliance Language
Always include: "This response is informational and does not constitute legal advice."

## Response Boundaries
Do not promise refunds. Do not share other customers' data. Do not override audit holds without analyst approval.
