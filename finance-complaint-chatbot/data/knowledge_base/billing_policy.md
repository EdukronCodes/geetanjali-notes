# Finance Billing & Dispute Policy

## Transaction Disputes
Customers may dispute transactions within 60 days of posting date. Required information: account number, transaction ID, and dispute reason.

## Billing Discrepancies
Common causes: duplicate charges, incorrect tax application, currency conversion errors. Resolution SLA: 5 business days for routine cases.

## Reconciliation Status
Monthly reconciliation closes on the 3rd business day. Status inquiries require account number and statement period (YYYY-MM).

## Payment Delays
Payment delays beyond 30 days trigger escalation to collections. Customers should provide payment reference and expected settlement date.

## Escalation Criteria
Escalate to human analyst when: amount exceeds $50,000, regulatory complaint mentioned, fraud suspected, or customer requests supervisor.
